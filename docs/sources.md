# Sources and licensing notes

Primary source: *Fabrication and Analysis of an Automated Drone Battery Swapping
and Charging System*, Kenyatta University, January 2025, co-authored by
Irungu Josphat Macharia and Israel Nyakoe Maragia.

- Chapter 3: 2-DOF servo arm, magnetic claw, ultrasonic docking detection,
  Arduino, 180° rotating battery platter and 3D-printed replicas.
- Table 3.2: historical upper arm 0.12 m and forearm 0.09 m.
- Tables 4.1 and 4.2: timing and outcomes of 20 reported trials.
- Chapter 4: 12 successful trials / 20 attempts, 60% reliability,
  approximately 90 s successful-trial average; alignment and gripping failures.
- Limitations: no real drone, no active charging, no outdoor validation.
