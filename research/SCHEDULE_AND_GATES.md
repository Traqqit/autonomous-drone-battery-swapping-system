# Illustrative eight-quarter program
Q1 literature, requirements and UAV/battery selection (G0).
Q2 architecture tradeoffs and CAD concepts (G1).
Q3 robot planning, control and fault simulation (G2).
Q4 inert-cartridge bench prototype (G3).
Q5 reliability and repeatability refinement (G3).
Q6 qualified charger and BMS integration (G4).
Q7 controlled UAV tests (G5).
Q8 analysis, release and papers (G6).
Gates depend on verification, not elapsed time.
