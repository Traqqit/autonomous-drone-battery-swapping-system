# Literature Review Protocol

## Questions
1. Which UAV energy replenishment approaches have been demonstrated?
2. What docking, alignment and exchange architectures are used?
3. What evidence exists for cycle time, reliability and unattended operation?
4. What remains unresolved for low-cost field-deployable systems?

## Search method
Search IEEE Xplore, Scopus, Web of Science, Google Scholar and publisher databases with:
`("UAV" OR drone OR multirotor) AND ("battery swapping" OR "battery exchange" OR "autonomous charging")`
Record database, query, search date, duplicates, eligibility and full-text availability.
Do not label this a systematic review until screening is actually performed.

## Seed references (to screen, not a completed review)
- Wu et al., *Autonomous Charging and Battery Swapping for Unmanned Aerial Vehicles: A Review*, Advanced Intelligent Systems, published 25 Oct 2026, DOI: 10.1002/aisy.70584. Surveys methods and unattended-operation tradeoffs.
- *Automatic Jig-Assisted Battery Exchange for Lightweight Drones*, Machines 2024, 12(11), 818, DOI: 10.3390/machines12110818. Robotic arm with positioning jig, prototype under two minutes.
- PX4 Precision Landing documentation: https://docs.px4.io/main/en/advanced_features/precland
- ArduPilot Battery Failsafe documentation: https://ardupilot.org/copter/docs/failsafe-battery.html
- 2025 university baseline report by the two research collaborators.

## Extraction fields
Architecture, UAV type, battery type, number of exchange trials, reliability,
cycle time definition, localization method, charge method, limitations,
whether the results are simulated or measured, DOI/URL, and review notes.
