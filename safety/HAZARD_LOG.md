# Preliminary hazard register (not a safety certification)
| Hazard | Consequence | Mitigation to investigate | Test |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rotor energizes during swap | injury | independent disarm interlock | fault injection |
| Battery dropped | damage | positive gripper retention | inert load test |
| Misaligned electrical contacts | short or damage | keyed connector and seat sensing | force/fit test |
| Damaged battery charged | thermal incident | BMS and thermal monitoring | qualified review |
| Arm unintended motion | pinch injury | guarding and E-stop | bench test |
| Power loss during transfer | stranded battery | passive retention and recovery | fault test |
Start mechanical testing with inert replicas. Energized lithium packs require electrical safety review.
